Security

How Corvus Forge protects your data

You trust us with evidence about your company's security. These are the commitments we make about how we handle it, and how to reach us if something looks wrong.

SOC 2 Type II audit in progress

Report a security problem

Email security@corvusforge.io to report a vulnerability, a suspected breach, or a concern about how we handle data. Please include what you found and how to reproduce it. We read every report, reply as soon as we can, and handle confirmed problems under our incident response plan. Researchers can also find our contact in our security.txt file.

Encryption

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Our website and email only accept encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections. Client files are stored with providers that encrypt data at rest, and the company laptop used for client work has full-disk encryption (BitLocker, AES 256-bit).

Who can access your data

  • Client work is done only on a company-managed laptop, never on personal devices.
  • Every account that can reach client data uses a unique password from a password manager and multi-factor authentication.
  • Access is limited to what an engagement needs, and we review who has access to what every quarter.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your evidence and working files for 6 months after an engagement ends, then delete them completely, including emptying them from the trash. Each deletion is logged twice: once when the files are deleted and once when we confirm they can't be recovered.

Breach notification

If we confirm an incident that affects your data, we tell you without undue delay and no later than 72 hours after confirming it, so you can meet your own obligations, including GDPR's 72-hour deadline.

AI tools

We may use AI tools to help with analysis. We only use business plans or accounts where model training on our inputs is turned off, so your data is never used to train AI models. Only the excerpts a task needs go into an AI tool, and a person checks every score and finding before you receive it.

Accurate results

Readiness scores are calculated by documented logic with automated tests. Every change to that logic is reviewed by a person before it's released.

Our own compliance

We follow the same program we help clients build: 16 written security policies, reviewed every 6 months, with automated daily checks. Our own SOC 2 Type II audit is in progress. We'll share the report with clients under NDA once an independent auditor issues it.
